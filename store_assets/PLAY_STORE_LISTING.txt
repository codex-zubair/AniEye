--- Full description (max 4000) ---
AniEye answers a question every fan faces: "What order should I watch this
in?" It is a quick, fully offline reference for the recommended viewing order
of well-known anime series, from long-running shows to multi-arc
universes.

Pick a series, and AniEye shows its ordered watch list — the seasons, arcs,
films and optional entries, numbered in the order you should watch them. No
internet, no accounts, no ads.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- 14 popular series with step-by-step watch orders.
- Over 85 numbered watch steps in total.
- Order types labelled clearly: release order, season order, part order,
  saga order and more.
- Optional steps (films, OVAs, spinoffs) flagged so you can skip them.
- Short notes explaining each step.
- Instant search to find a series fast.

FEATURED SERIES
Monogatari, Fate, Haruhi Suzumiya, JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, Gintama,
One Piece, Studio Ghibli, Mob Psycho 100, Oregairu, Steins;Gate,
Neon Genesis Evangelion, Naruto, Attack on Titan and Frieren.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open AniEye and browse or search for a series.
2. Tap it to see its numbered watch order.
3. Follow the steps top to bottom; skip anything marked Optional.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No internet permission at all — the app cannot go online.
- Zero Android permissions requested.
- No ads, no analytics, no trackers.
- The whole guide is bundled inside the app.
- The app stores no data at all — nothing is saved.

EDITORIAL CONTENT
Watch orders are original editorial suggestions, not official guides. They
are provided to help you enjoy a series in a sensible order. AniEye contains
no video, audio, manga or other copyrighted media, and no verbatim
quotations, dialogue or lyrics.

Open source
AniEye's source code is public at
https://github.com/codex-zubair/AniEye

Built by codex-zubair.
