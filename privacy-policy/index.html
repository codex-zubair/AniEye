AniEye

Offline Anime Watch-Order Helper · codex-zubair

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Overview

AniEye (“the App”) is a free, ad-free, fully offline anime watch-order helper for Android, published by codex-zubair (“we”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect when you use it. By using AniEye you agree to this policy.

In one sentence: AniEye collects no personal data, contains no advertising or analytics, requests no permissions, and never connects to the internet.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

  • No account, sign-in, or registration is required.
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts are collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses are collected.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools are used.
  • We do not know which series you look up.

3. Internet Access

AniEye does not request or use the Android INTERNET permission. The App is technically incapable of connecting to the internet. The entire watch-order guide is bundled inside the App; nothing is downloaded, streamed, uploaded or synced.

4. Permissions

AniEye requests no Android permissions at all. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, storage, contacts, phone, SMS, or network. The only optional interaction with an external app is opening the GitHub or Privacy Policy links on the About screen, which opens your web browser only when you explicitly tap them.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

AniEye stores no data at all. Your search text and the pages you open exist only while the App is running and are discarded when it closes. Nothing is written to disk, and there is no history or profile.

6. Third Parties

The App contains no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The App uses the open-source url_launcher plugin solely to open links you explicitly tap. No data is shared with anyone.

7. Children's Privacy

AniEye is a general-audience reference app suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

8. Data Security

Since no data leaves your device and none is transmitted, there is no data for us to secure or for a third party to breach.

9. Content

AniEye's watch orders and notes are original editorial suggestions, not official guides. The App contains no video, audio, manga or other copyrighted media, and no verbatim quotations, dialogue or lyrics.

10. Open Source

AniEye's complete source code is public at github.com/codex-zubair/AniEye. Anyone may inspect the code to verify these privacy claims.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in a future app update and published on this page.
